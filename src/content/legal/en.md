## 4. Content sources

### Bible

- **Bible text**: Crampon Bible (1923 edition, public domain), adapted and modernized for the app (divine name “Yahweh” replaced, vocatives in the Psalms revised).

### Liturgy

- **Mass readings and Liturgy of the Hours** (ordinary form): texts of the Association épiscopale liturgique pour les pays francophones (AELF), retrieved through its API (api.aelf.org). © AELF, all rights reserved.
- **Liturgical calendar, traditional Mass and Office (1962)**: provided by the public CatéGPT API (categpt.chat), which itself uses texts from AELF (ordinary form) and Divinum Officium (extraordinary form). The source of each text is shown on screen.

### Parishes and addresses

- **Parishes**: data from open-church.io. The list of churches comes from Wikidata (CC0).
- **City search and geolocation**: Base Adresse Nationale (api-adresse.data.gouv.fr), under the Etalab Open Licence 2.0.

### Content written for Bibliae

- **Saints, timeline, figures and genealogy**: written for Bibliae based on Church tradition and the biblical text. Dates are indicative, from the traditional chronology.
- **Catechism**: paraphrases written for Bibliae, not quotations from the Catechism of the Catholic Church. The official text is available at vatican.va (© Libreria Editrice Vaticana).
- **Prayers**: traditional public-domain texts, with their Latin, Greek and Aramaic versions.

### Illustrations and fonts

- **Saint illustrations**: © DolceDreamsStudio, used with their permission.
- **Fonts**: Cormorant Garamond, EB Garamond and Inter, under the SIL Open Font License.
