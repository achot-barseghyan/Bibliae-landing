## 1. Who we are

The Bibliae app (Android and iOS) is designed and published personally by **Adrien Barseghyan**, an independent developer. There is no company behind the app.

Data controller and privacy contact: Adrien Barseghyan, [privacy@bibliae.app](mailto:privacy@bibliae.app).

## 2. Data we process

We only process what is needed for the features you use.

| Data | Purpose | Where | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| Notes, highlights, underlines, bookmarks | Let you annotate the Bible | On your device only | Until you delete them |
| Reading progress, plans, rosary | Resume where you left off | On your device only | Until you delete them |
| Preferences (text size, calendar, language) | Adapt the display | On your device only | Until you delete them |
| Location (precise or approximate) | Find nearby parishes, only when you tap “Near me” | Sent to the Base Adresse Nationale and open-church.io for the search, never stored by Bibliae | Duration of the request |
| Parish search (name, city, postal code) | Show results | open-church.io and Base Adresse Nationale | Not stored by Bibliae |
| Date requested for the liturgy | Show the day's texts | AELF and CatéGPT | Not stored by Bibliae |
| Technical data (IP address, device model, app version) | Security and reliable operation | Logs of our host and the services called | 30 days at our host |
| Push token (if you enable notifications) | Send the saint of the day or prayer reminders | Apple / Google notification services | Until you turn them off |
| Anonymous crash reports (if a tool is used) | Fix bugs | Tool name | 90 days |
| Website contact form (name, email, message) | Reply to you | Delivered by Resend to our inbox | As long as needed to handle your request, 3 years at most |

## 3. What we don't do

- We **do not sell** your data or share it with third parties for commercial purposes.
- We **show no advertising** and do not use the Android Advertising ID (AAID) or IDFA.
- We **do not collect** persistent device identifiers (IMEI, IMSI, SIM serial number).
- We **do not access** your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or SMS.
- We do not track your location **in the background**.
- We do not build any profile of your religious practice. Your notes and readings stay on your device and we cannot see them.

## 4. Permissions

Each permission is requested **when you use the related feature**, after a screen explaining why. You can refuse: the rest of the app keeps working.

- **Location**: for “Near me” in “Find a parish”. Message shown before the request: “Bibliae uses your location to show parishes near you, only when you ask.”
- **Notifications**: for the saint of the day and the prayer reminders you choose.
- **Internet**: to download texts, the daily liturgy and parish information.

## 5. Sharing with third parties

We use a few service providers only to run the service. They may not use your data for their own purposes.

- **Server hosting**: Vercel Inc. (United States), certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
- **Contact form delivery**: Resend (United States). Only your name, email and message are sent to it, to deliver them to us.
- **Contact form spam protection**: Cloudflare Turnstile (Cloudflare, Inc., United States), certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. On the contact page only, it checks technical browser data (including your IP address) to tell humans from bots, without advertising cookies or tracking.
- **Liturgical texts**: AELF (api.aelf.org) and CatéGPT (categpt.chat). Only the requested date is sent.
- **Parishes**: open-church.io. **Addresses and geolocation**: Base Adresse Nationale (api-adresse.data.gouv.fr, a French government service). Only the search text or your position at the time of the search is sent, never your identity.
- **Notifications**: Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google), if you enable notifications.
- **Crash reporting tool, if used**.

We may also disclose data when required by law. Where a provider is located outside the European Union, transfers are covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the Data Privacy Framework.

## 6. Security

All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS / TLS). Data saved on your device is protected by the operating system's encryption (Android and iOS). Access to our servers is restricted to the people who need it.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Your local data (notes, highlights, progress) stays as long as you keep it. To erase everything:

- in the app: **Settings › My data › Delete all my data**;
- or uninstall the app, which removes everything it stored on the device.
- Our server's technical logs are deleted automatically after 30 days.

### Account and account deletion

Bibliae **does not require an account**. If an account is added later (sync across devices), it can be deleted at any time in the app, Settings › Account › Delete my account, and from the web page bibliae.app/delete-account. All data linked to the account will be permanently deleted within 30 days; an account is never merely deactivated.

## 8. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to and port your data.

To exercise these rights, email [privacy@bibliae.app](mailto:privacy@bibliae.app). We reply within one month. Since most of your data stays on your device, we do not hold it and you can delete it yourself at any time.

You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority; in France, the CNIL ([www.cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr/)).

## 9. Legal basis

- **Performance of the service** you request: displaying texts, searching parishes.
- **Consent**: location and notifications, which you can withdraw at any time in your phone settings.
- **Legitimate interest**: service security and technical logs.

## 10. Children

Bibliae is for a general audience and is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 15 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 11. Changes

We may update this policy, for example if a new feature uses other data. The date at the top shows the latest version. For significant changes, we will inform you in the app before they apply.
